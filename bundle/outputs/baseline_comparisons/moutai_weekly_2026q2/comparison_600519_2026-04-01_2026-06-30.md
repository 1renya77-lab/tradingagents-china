## Reading

- `agent` tests the dynamic target-position signal generated by TradingAgents.
- `buy_hold_100` buys the stock once at the first executable trading step and then holds it without rebalancing.
- `macd` uses a classic MACD long/cash rule.
- `kdj_rsi` combines KDJ trend confirmation with RSI momentum filter.
- `zmr` is a z-score / Bollinger-band mean-reversion baseline.
- `sma` is a moving-average trend-following baseline.
- `win_rate` is the share of non-flat daily strategy returns that are positive.
- Compare total return together with Sharpe and max drawdown; strong return with much larger drawdown may still be undesirable.
